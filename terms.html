Split & Shared

When you split an expense or join a group, the people involved can see the amounts, names and balances tied to it. Balances are a record of what you and your friends agree is owed; settling up happens between you outside the app, and we are not a party to any payment. Comments, payments and receipt photos you add are visible to the others in the group too. Only add people you know.

Collaborate

Collaborate lets two or three people who choose to join see each other's spending by category for each month. It is started by an admin, who invites people with a one-time code and can remove them or close the group. By joining you agree that, for each category and each month from the one you join, the others in the group will see the amount you spent (refunds netted off), and nothing else: no transactions, merchants, descriptions, income, balances or net worth. You can keep any category out of it, and you can leave at any time, which takes your figures out of the combined view at once. We cannot take back what another member has already seen or noted down, so share only what you are comfortable sharing, with people you know. Closing your account removes you from the group.
