Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Who we are: Praveen Rana, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). Finease is a personal-finance app that helps you track spending, budgets, investments, loans and shared expenses. We decide why and how your personal data is used, which makes us the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Contact: privacy@finease.co.in — for any question about your data, or to ask us to delete it.

This policy explains what Finease collects, why, who else sees it, and the choices you have. It describes what the app does today.

What we collect

Account details. Your name, email and mobile number, your UPI ID if you add one, and — if you sign in with Google — the name and email on your Google account. Your password and PIN (one PIN for the app lock and the Vault) are stored only as one-way hashes, so we can't read them back.

Financial information you enter or import. Accounts and balances, credit cards, transactions, budgets, recurring bills, goals, investments and holdings (including statements you import), loans, assets, insurance policies, and your monthly income and pay day if you give them.

Statements and holdings files you import (PDF, Excel or CSV). The file is sent to our server and read there in memory. The file itself is not kept (its name is, with the import); only the transactions or holdings in it are stored, for you to review. If a file is password-protected, the password you type is used only to open it and is not stored. A statement row loses its description (names, UPI ids) the moment you approve or discard it, keeping only its date, amount, reference number and link to the entry, so a statement uploaded again is still recognised. Rows you have not reviewed within 7 days are discarded and cleared. The holdings read from a holdings file are cleared once you apply or discard the import, or after 7 days if you have not reviewed them. You can delete any import from the import screen.

Bank SMS (only if you turn on SMS sync). The app reads your inbox on your phone and picks out messages that look like bank or card transaction or due-date alerts. Only those — the sender, the text and the time — are sent to our servers, to suggest transactions for you to review. We filter out one-time passwords and messages from personal numbers on your phone before anything is sent, and again on our server. These filters are automatic and cannot be perfect: if we find a message that contains a one-time password we delete it, and you can discard any message in the app, which deletes its text at once. Other messages stay on your phone. While SMS sync is on, Finease also checks each new message on your phone as it arrives, using the same filter, so it can tell you a new transaction is waiting for you to review, even when the app is closed. Messages that are not bank alerts are ignored at once and are not stored, recorded or sent anywhere, and the notification does not show the amount, merchant or sender. This check does not save or send any message: a bank message goes to our servers only when Finease scans your inbox for suggestions, as described here. Turning off SMS sync stops this immediately. Uploaded message text is deleted as soon as you approve or discard the message, and in any case after 3 days if you have not reviewed it; a card statement message waiting for its card to be added is kept for up to 7 days, so its due date can be applied if you add the card in that time. When the text is deleted we keep what was read from it: the sender, amount, merchant, the last four digits of the account or card, any balance the message stated, and a one-way fingerprint of the message so the same message is not suggested twice. A transaction is added only when you approve it. A balance, or a card due amount and date, stated in a message is applied to the matching account or card straight away and shown as an estimate, because it is the bank's own figure; check it against your statement. A transaction you approved stays in your account. You can switch SMS sync off, or remove the permission, at any time.

Contacts (only if you allow it, for Split & Shared). The phone numbers in your address book — not the names — are sent to our server to check which people already use Finease. We don't save them. For contacts who are registered on Finease, we also save the name you have saved them under so Split & Shared can show each person to you by that name; it is visible only to you and is deleted with your account. Names of contacts who aren't on Finease never leave your phone.

Microphone (only if you use voice expense). Your phone's speech-recognition service (Google's, on most Android phones) turns your voice into text. We receive only the text you confirm, not the recording. To help it recognise your words, the app also gives that service the names of your categories, banks and cards, but never account numbers.

Reports. If you report a person, we keep the reason and any note you write, with your account, so we can review it. The person is not told who reported them.

Photos and camera (only if you attach a receipt). You can pick a photo or take one with the camera. It is stored privately and only you and the people in that group can see it. A single photo cannot be removed from the app: it stays with the expense, and is deleted with your account unless it is attached to a shared expense (see deleting your data below).

Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by your phone. We never receive biometric data.

Notifications (only if you allow them). Reminders for bills, EMIs and the weekly check-in are set up and shown by your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us. Split activity notifications are different. They are off until you switch them on in Profile › Notifications › Split activity, and they are sent from our server through Expo's push service and Google's notification service, so the text of each one passes through those services. It can contain a person's name, a group's name and, only if you switch amounts on (they are off by default), an amount. We store your phone's push address against your account to send them; it is deleted when you sign out or switch them off, and when you delete your account. You can turn notifications off in Profile › Notifications or in your phone's settings.

Technical information. Our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as the time and IP address, for security. If the app crashes or you send a problem report, we receive technical details such as the device model, Android and app version and what went wrong, and in a problem report whatever you write. To slow down password and PIN guessing we keep short-lived counters of attempts (for up to an hour) against an IP address, email, mobile number or account. We also keep a short security log of sign-ins and failures, PIN and password changes and resets, Vault unlocks, accepting these documents, blocking or reporting a person, and deleting an account. It holds an account id, the kind of event, whether it worked and the time, with no IP address, device detail or financial data, and each entry is deleted after 180 days.

Your consent. We keep a record of which version of this policy and the Terms you accepted, that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when. We also record, with its version and time, each optional agreement that is asked for on its own (reading bank messages, joining a Collaborate group).

How we use it

To run the app: show your numbers, categorise spending, suggest transactions from bank messages, work out budgets and what's due, let you share expenses with friends, send account emails (welcome, verification, password reset, security notices) and the notifications you switch on, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We don't show ads, we don't build advertising profiles, and we don't sell your data.

Who else can see it

  • People you share with. Expenses, payments, comments, receipt photos, balances and names you put into a Split & Shared group or expense are visible to the others in it.
  • People in your Collaborate group. If you join a collaboration (up to three people), the others in it see each category's total for the month and your part of it. They never see your transactions, merchants, income or balances. You can keep any category out of it, and leave at any time, which takes your figures out of the group straight away. Joining is recorded as your agreement, and the one-time invite code is stored only as a hash.
  • Other Finease users who have your number. If someone has your mobile number in their contacts and uses the contacts feature, they can see that you're on Finease and the name on your profile. You can block a person from Profile › Blocked people.
  • Services that process data for us, only to run the app: Render (our servers), Supabase (our database and receipt photos), both hosted in Singapore; Resend (account emails, processed in Tokyo, Japan); Sentry (crash reports, processed in the United States); Expo and Google (push notifications, if you switch Split activity on); and Google (Sign-In, and speech recognition if you use voice expense). Some of these providers process data outside India.
  • The law. If a valid legal order requires it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. PINs and passwords are stored as one-way hashes. The Vault (assets, loans, insurance) needs your PIN and locks after inactivity. Signing in on a phone asks for your PIN, and resetting a password or PIN signs out your other phones. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a PIN that is hard to guess and keep your phone locked.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. To delete it, go to Profile › Delete account in the app (it asks for your PIN). That permanently deletes your transactions, accounts, cards, budgets, investments, loans, assets, goals and settings, and removes your name, mobile number, email, UPI ID and sign-in details. That includes your imported statements, consent records and receipt photos. Expenses you shared in a group stay for the other people in it, with the payments, comments and changes you made, shown as "Deleted user", because they are part of their records too; a receipt photo attached to a shared expense stays with that expense for the same reason. What is left of your account is an anonymous record with no name, email, number or sign-in details; security log entries, which hold only an account id, are deleted within 180 days. Copies in backups are removed when the backups are overwritten, within 7 days.

If you can't open the app, follow the steps on the delete-account page or email privacy@finease.co.in; we will delete your data within 15 days.

Your choices and rights

You can use Finease without SMS, contacts, microphone or camera access, and you can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings at any time. In the app, turning off Sync bank SMS stops the phone reading messages at once (saved message text is then deleted on the schedule above), each statement import can be deleted from the import screen, notifications can be turned off in Profile › Notifications, and you can leave a Collaborate group at any time. You can withdraw your consent to this policy by deleting your account (Profile › Delete account). Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to know what data we hold, to have it corrected or erased, to nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot, and to have a grievance answered. Our grievance contact is Praveen Rana, privacy@finease.co.in. We acknowledge complaints promptly and aim to resolve them within 30 days; if you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Finease is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or how it is used, we'll update this page and the date above and ask you to review and accept the new version in the app before you continue.
