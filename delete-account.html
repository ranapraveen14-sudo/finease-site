Delete your Finease account

App: Finease · Publisher: Praveen Rana

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Finease and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your PIN to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't open the app: request deletion here

Fill in this form. We will email you at the address you give to confirm it is you, and then delete your account and its data within 15 days. You do not need the app.

What is deleted

  • Your transactions, accounts, credit cards, budgets, recurring bills, goals, investments, loans, assets and insurance records.
  • Your name, mobile number, email address, UPI ID and sign-in details.
  • Your imported statements and the rows read from them, your consent records, and your phone's notification address.
  • Receipt photos you uploaded, unless they are attached to a shared expense (see below).

What stays

  • Expenses you shared in a group stay for the other people in that group, with the payments, comments and changes you made, shown as "Deleted user", because they are part of those people's records too. A receipt photo attached to a shared expense stays with that expense.
  • Security log. Entries hold only an account id, with no name, and are deleted within 180 days.
  • Backups. Copies in backups are removed when the backups are overwritten, within 7 days.

Deletion can't be undone. See the privacy policy for details.
